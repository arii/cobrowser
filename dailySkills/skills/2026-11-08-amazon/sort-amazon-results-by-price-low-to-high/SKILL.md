# Sort Amazon results by Price Low to High

Change result ordering from Featured to Price Low to High to compare cheapest options first.

**When to use:** Use when browsing Amazon search results and you want cheapest items first.

**Sites:** https://www.amazon.com/

## Steps

1. Click SELECT "Sort by: Featured" — Open the sort order menu to choose how results are ordered.
2. Click BUTTON "Price: Low to High" — Select Price Low to High ordering for results.
